Basic RimWorld priorities, in the order they usually matter. Treat them as rules of thumb, not law.

Survival first
- Food comes before everything else: keep at least 4 days of food in the stockpile, and 8 or more before winter or a long growing break. Cooks on priority 1, growers on priority 1 or 2, a hunter on priority 2 when meat is short. Raw food and meals are not the same: a meal is worth about twice the nutrition and feeds more people per cook.
- Beds next: one bed per colonist, in a closed room, before anything else comfortable. Sleeping on the ground or outside costs mood and health.
- Medicine and rest: a downed or ill colonist needs a doctor on priority 1 and a bed. Keep Doctor and Patient on priority 1 or 2 for at least one capable colonist.
- Defence: before the first raid (around day 5 to 15) have at least two armed colonists with the best weapons and some cover. Draft the shooters when a raid starts, and undraft them when it ends so they go back to work. Do not send a weak colonist to melee a raider.
- Heat and cold: keep colonists out of extreme weather. Stay indoors in cold snaps and heat waves.

Work priorities
- Priority 1 is done first, 4 last, 0 is off. Give each colonist their best two or three skills on 1 or 2, put the essentials (Firefighter, Patient, Doctor, Cooking, Growing, Hauling, Cleaning) on someone, and make sure every work type has at least one worker.
- Passions matter: a colonist with a major passion in a skill is happier and faster doing it, so favour those jobs.
- Do not leave a skill the colony needs uncovered. If the only cook is hurt, move Cooking to the next best colonist.
- A colonist who cannot do a kind of work (shown as cannot) never gets it.
- Hauling and Cleaning on 3 or 4 for most people keeps the colony tidy without costing much time.

Research
- Early: Electricity unlocks light, cooling and many machines; then Batteries, Smithing, and Complex clothing for the winter. Choose the project that solves the current need (cold: clothing; food: irrigation or hydroponics later). One researcher with a high Intellectual skill and a research bench is enough early on.

Mood and people
- Watch mood: below about 35 a colonist is at risk of a mental break. Fix the cause (a bed, food that fits their policy, a better schedule, a break to joy) before the break, and put a stressed colonist on lighter work.
- Schedules: sleep about 8 hours, one or two hours of joy in the evening, the rest work. A colonist who is tired sleeps badly and works worse.
- Letters and quests: accept a quest only when the colony can spare the people and the reward is worth it; reject one that needs defenders when raids are likely. Letters that expire need an answer before they do.

Use the goals board. A short list of two or three goals you can finish this season beats a long list.
